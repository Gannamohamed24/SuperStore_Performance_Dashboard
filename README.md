# 📊 Superstore Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Excel Business Intelligence Dashboard** built to analyze sales, profitability, customer behavior, regional performance, shipping operations, and discount impact using the Superstore dataset.

The project consists of **two interactive dashboards** and a dedicated **Executive Insights Report**.

---

## 📈 Dashboards

### 1. Executive Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of business performance through:

* Total Sales, Profit & Profit Margin
* Total Orders, Quantity & Customers
* Monthly Sales Trends
* Yearly Sales & Profit
* Sales by Category
* Top 10 States by Sales
* Regional Performance

**Filters:** Order Date, Shipping Mode, Customer Segment, Product Category

### 2. Customer & Operations Dashboard

Focuses on customer behavior and operational performance:

* Average Discount & Shipping Days
* Average Order Value & Profit per Order
* Returning Customers
* Top Customers by Sales
* Most & Least Profitable Cities
* Customer Segment Analysis
* Shipping Performance
* Discount Impact on Sales & Profit
* Best-Selling Products

### 3. Executive Insights Report

A dedicated report summarizing the major findings across:

* Regional Performance
* Category Performance
* Discount Impact
* Customer Segments
* Shipping
* Customer & State Performance
* Sales & Profit Trends
